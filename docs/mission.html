Mission

What is Right to Repair?

The right to repair movement is an advocacy effort aimed at preventing manufacturers from creating barriers that restrict how consumers and independent businesses fix their devices. It seeks to establish legislation that requires companies to provide open access to necessary spare parts, diagnostic tools, and service manuals. Fundamentally, the movement operates on the principle that ownership of a product should inherently include the freedom to repair it without being forced to rely exclusively on the original manufacturer.

How do we identify with RtR?

As a team, we are feel very strongly about the right to repair movement. We are all DIYers in one way or another, and we do feel the effects of companies putting up barriers for repair. So, we wanted to address this problem with our project.

The way we did this was by first declaring how we wanted to support this movement. We decided that we wanted to focus on the “Daily Fight” over the “Legal Right”. To us, this meant that we wanted to find a way to relieve some of the daily stress of this problem in RtR, rather than addressing the legal battle going on between companies and people.
